# SnapCard Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 14, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how All Good Things Always, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the SnapCard mobile application ("SnapCard").

## 1. Privacy at a glance

SnapCard is designed as an offline screenshot organizer. It does not require an account, operate an advertising service, or upload the contents of your screenshots, recognized text, cards, or voice notes to servers operated by us. Those items are processed or stored in the app's private storage on your device unless you deliberately export a backup or open/share content through another app.

## 2. Information handled on your device

At your direction, SnapCard may access and process screenshots or photos you select, photographs you take, recognized text, cards, fields, favorites, reminders, voice notes, app preferences, and backup archives. This content can include personal information depending on what appears in it. SnapCard processes and stores it locally for the features you request. We do not receive or maintain a copy on our servers.

## 3. Permissions

- **Photos:** to read an image you select for on-device recognition.
- **Camera:** to photograph information you choose to recognize.
- **Microphone:** to record a voice note after you start recording from a card. SnapCard does not record in the background.
- **Notifications:** to deliver a reminder you schedule.

You can deny or revoke permissions in device settings. The related feature may stop working, but the rest of SnapCard remains available.

## 4. On-device recognition and SDK diagnostics

SnapCard uses Google ML Kit for text recognition. Image and text inputs and recognition results are processed on-device and are not sent to Google. The ML Kit SDK may periodically contact Google to obtain fixes, model updates, or hardware compatibility information, and may send diagnostic and usage metrics such as device and app information, per-installation identifiers, performance measurements, API configuration, feature event types, and error codes. Google states that this diagnostic data is encrypted in transit and is not transferred to third parties. Google handles that information under its own privacy terms.

SnapCard does not use the Google Advertising ID to serve personalized ads.

## Google Advertising ID and analytics

On Android devices where it is available, SnapCard may access the Google Advertising ID (also called the Android Advertising ID or AAID) and transmit it to Google Analytics for Firebase together with limited analytics data, such as app version, device and operating-system information, feature interactions, session events, performance information, and crash or diagnostic data. We use this information only to measure aggregate usage, understand feature adoption, monitor app performance, diagnose problems, prevent duplicate counting, and improve SnapCard.

We do not use the Advertising ID to read, identify, or transmit the contents of screenshots, recognized text, cards, voice notes, or backups. We do not use it for personalized advertising, sell it, or link it to a name, email address, account, or non-resettable device identifier.

The Advertising ID is provided by Google Play services and can be reset or deleted through Android's **Settings > Privacy > Ads** controls. If you delete the Advertising ID, Android may return a string of zeros instead. Analytics information is protected in transit and retained only for the period configured for the analytics service and reasonably necessary for the purposes described above. Google's handling of analytics information is governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy policy.

## 5. Exports, links, and platform services

The in-app Privacy Policy and User Agreement pages are hosted by GitHub and loaded over the internet in an embedded system WebView. When you open either page, GitHub may receive ordinary web-request information such as your IP address, browser or device user agent, request time, and cookies according to GitHub's privacy practices. SnapCard does not add tracking scripts to these pages.

If you export a backup, use a system share sheet, open a saved link, or otherwise send content to another app or service, your chosen destination may receive that content and its privacy policy applies. Apple, Google, and your device or app-store provider may independently process purchase, download, crash, or device information under their own terms; we do not control those platform services.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Cards, local image copies, voice notes, and settings remain on your device until you delete an item, use **Settings > Delete all data**, uninstall SnapCard, or the operating system removes app data. Deleting a card also deletes its associated local image and voice note. Exported backups remain wherever you saved them and must be deleted there separately. Because we do not hold your local content on our servers, we cannot retrieve or delete it remotely.

## 7. Security

SnapCard stores content within the application sandbox and uses operating-system permission controls. No storage method is completely secure. Protect your device with a passcode, keep its software current, and keep exported backups in a trusted location.

## 8. Children

SnapCard is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information on our servers. If you believe a child has provided information directly to us, contact us.

## 9. Your privacy choices

You control what images and recordings are added, can edit or delete individual cards and voice notes, can delete all in-app data, and can revoke permissions through system settings. We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for targeted advertising. Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide additional rights. For information you send directly to us, contact us below.

## 10. International use

We are based in the United States. If you contact us, your communication may be processed in the United States where privacy laws may differ from those in your location.

## 11. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes to SnapCard, SDK behavior, or legal requirements. We will revise the effective date and make the updated policy available here. Material changes will be disclosed in the app or release notes when appropriate.
