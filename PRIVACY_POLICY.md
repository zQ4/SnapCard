# SnapCard Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 14, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how All Good Things Always, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the SnapCard mobile application ("SnapCard").

## 1. Privacy at a glance

SnapCard is a screenshot organizer that does not require an account. Screenshot recognition, cards, and voice notes are processed or stored in the app's private storage on your device unless you deliberately export, open, or share content. The Android app uses limited network services for installation attribution, analytics, configuration, and messaging as described below. These services do not receive screenshot contents, recognized card text, or local voice notes from SnapCard's organizer features.

## 2. Information handled on your device

At your direction, SnapCard may access and process:

- screenshots or photos you select, or photographs you take in the app;
- text recognized from those images and the cards, fields, favorites, and reminders you create;
- voice notes you choose to record for a card;
- app preferences, including language, appearance, and onboarding status; and
- a backup archive you choose to create or restore.

This content can include personal information depending on what appears in a screenshot or recording. SnapCard processes and stores it locally for the features you request. We do not receive or maintain a copy on our servers.

## 3. Permissions

SnapCard requests permissions only when relevant to a feature:

- **Photos:** to read an image you select for on-device recognition.
- **Camera:** to photograph information you choose to recognize.
- **Microphone:** to record a voice note after you start recording from a card. SnapCard does not record in the background.
- **Notifications:** to deliver a reminder you schedule.

You can deny or revoke these permissions in your device settings. The related feature may stop working, but the rest of SnapCard remains available.

## 4. On-device recognition and SDK diagnostics

SnapCard uses Google ML Kit for text recognition. Image and text inputs and recognition results are processed on-device and are not sent to Google. The ML Kit SDK may periodically contact Google to obtain fixes, model updates, or hardware compatibility information, and may send diagnostic and usage metrics such as device and app information, per-installation identifiers, performance measurements, API configuration, feature event types, and error codes. Google states that this diagnostic data is encrypted in transit and is not transferred to third parties. Google handles that information under its own privacy terms.

SnapCard does not use the Google Advertising ID to serve personalized ads.

## Google Advertising ID, attribution, and analytics

On supported Android devices, SnapCard may access the Google Advertising ID (also called the Android Advertising ID or AAID). SnapCard may send the Advertising ID, Adjust advertising identifier, installation-attribution information, app version, device model and manufacturer, operating-system version, and request status to our attribution service and to Adjust. We use this information for installation attribution, aggregate data analysis, preventing duplicate measurement, understanding product usage, diagnosing problems, and improving SnapCard.

We do not use the Advertising ID to read, identify, or transmit the contents of screenshots, recognized text, cards, voice notes, or backups. We do not use it for personalized advertising, sell it, or link it to a name, email address, account, or non-resettable device identifier.

The Advertising ID is provided by Google Play services and can be reset or deleted through Android's **Settings > Privacy > Ads** controls. If you delete the Advertising ID, Android may return a string of zeros instead. Attribution and analytics information is transmitted using encrypted network connections and retained according to our service configuration and the applicable provider's retention settings. Adjust and Google process information under their respective privacy terms.

## Firebase messaging and installation information

The Android app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to support notifications. Firebase may automatically create and process a Firebase Installation ID, app version, Firebase user-agent information, and a messaging registration token so that messages can be delivered to a specific app installation. When a supported web feature requests notification status, SnapCard may provide the registration token, permission state, and app package name to that feature. Firebase and Google handle this information under their applicable privacy terms. SnapCard does not place screenshot contents, recognized text, cards, or voice recordings into Firebase messaging tokens.

## 5. Online content, exports, links, and platform services

On Android, SnapCard may request configuration from our service and may open web content returned by that service in an in-app WebView. A website loaded in the WebView may receive ordinary web-request information, including IP address, user agent, request time, cookies, and information you submit to the website. If the website requests a supported native feature, SnapCard may provide notification or media-permission status through the in-app JavaScript bridge. The website's displayed privacy notice and terms apply to its own collection and use of information.

The in-app Privacy Policy and User Agreement pages are hosted by GitHub and loaded over the internet in an embedded system WebView. When you open either page, GitHub may receive ordinary web-request information such as your IP address, browser or device user agent, request time, and cookies according to GitHub's privacy practices. SnapCard does not add tracking scripts to these pages.

If you export a backup, use a system share sheet, open a saved link, or otherwise send content to another app or service, your chosen destination may receive that content. Its privacy policy then applies. Apple, Google, and your device or app-store provider may independently process purchase, download, crash, or device information under their own terms; we do not control those platform services.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Cards, local image copies, voice notes, and settings remain on your device until you delete an item, use **Settings > Delete all data**, uninstall SnapCard, or the operating system removes app data. Deleting a card also deletes its associated local image and voice note. Exported backups remain wherever you chose to save them and must be deleted there separately. Because we do not hold your local content on our servers, we cannot retrieve or delete it remotely.

## 7. Security

SnapCard stores content within the application sandbox and uses operating-system permission controls. No storage method is completely secure. Protect your device with a passcode, keep its software current, and keep exported backups in a trusted location.

## 8. Children

SnapCard is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information on our servers. If you believe a child has provided information directly to us, contact us so we can address it.

## 9. Your privacy choices

You control what images and recordings are added, can edit or delete individual cards and voice notes, can delete all in-app data, and can revoke permissions through system settings. We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for targeted advertising.

Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide additional rights. Because app content remains on your device, those rights are generally exercised through SnapCard and device controls. For information you send directly to us, you may contact us using the details below.

## 10. International use

We are based in the United States. If you contact us, your communication may be processed in the United States where privacy laws may differ from those in your location.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to SnapCard, SDK behavior, or legal requirements. We will revise the effective date and make the updated policy available at the same public location. Material changes will be disclosed in the app or release notes when appropriate.
